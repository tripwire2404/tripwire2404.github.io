About The Website & The Session

Welcome! This website celebrates an incredible collaborative session between Ebanks and former worldstar R. Kelly. It's dedicated to sharing information, updates, and music from this legendary 6.5-hour creative marathon.

MAIN IDEA

What is the session and why is it so important?

The session was an epic collaborative musical event featuring Ebanks and R. Kelly, all taking place the night before the latter's trial with many guest rappers and singers joining in. Talk about an all-star lineup!

The significance? Over 150 songs came out of this session! Two creative minds came together and produced an absolutely massive amount of material. That's basically an entire discography in one afternoon.

Why haven't these songs appeared on Ebanks' records? (Do i really need to mention R. Kelly?

Due to the nature of R. Kelly's trial, Ebanks is legally not allowed to release these songs for an undisclosed period. Presumably when R. Kelly gets out of jail or he dies.

We're not even sure Ebanks will even REMEMBER the songs by that time.

Why Do All Of This?

Initially, I planned to keep these songs private after receiving them from a colleague who had access to the recordings. However, since Ebanks decided he wants to be Playboi Carti and doesn't want to release anything until he drops his cover of Biggie's "I Love The Dough" when Tay-K gets out of jail, I decided to do this out of spite.
